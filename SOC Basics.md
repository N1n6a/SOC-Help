As a SOC Analyst, you should know how to document— It's one of the, if not most, needed skill in the field.
A good documentation can save loads of time for Tier 2 Analysts (In case it needs escalation). A bad one will ruin your work, their work, and can cause more damage than the attack itself.

If you're a weathered Analyst, you can skip this. Or not. Everyone needs some remembrance of the basics sometimes.
